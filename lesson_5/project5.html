FOOD

Food is any substance that is consumed by an organism for nutritional support. Food usually consists of plant, animal, or fungal origin and contains essential nutrients such as carbohydrates, fats, proteins, vitamins, or minerals.

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), about 600 million people worldwide get sick, and 420,000 die each year, from eating contaminated food.

NUTRITION TIPS

Here are some nutition tips

  • Eat mostly whole, minimally processed foods: Emphasize vegetables, fruits, legumes, whole grains, nuts, and seeds. These foods are nutrient‑dense and lower in calories.
  • Include adequate protein: Choose seafood, poultry, eggs, beans, lentils, soy, nuts, and seeds. Limit processed meats.
  • Use healthy fats: Prefer olive, sunflower, or canola oil instead of solid fats high in saturated fat
  • Limit added sugars: Reduce sugary drinks, flavored syrups, and processed snacks. Choose foods with little or no added sugars.
  • Reduce sodium intake: Taste food before salting and choose low‑sodium canned or frozen options.
  • Avoid ultra‑processed foods: These contribute to weight gain and poor metabolic health. Choose whole or minimally processed alternatives.

Quick Health Tips

  • Eat 5 fruits and veggies every day
  • Drink 8 glasses of water daily
